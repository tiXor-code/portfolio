1. About this service

Retro Many Money is a personal content channel publishing short-form retro gaming clips on TikTok and Instagram. The associated developer integration ("Retro Games Clipper") is an internal tool operated solely by the channel owner, used to upload pre-edited gameplay clips from a local desktop pipeline to the owner's own TikTok account. It is not a third-party service offered to other users.

2. Who can use it

The integration accepts only a single authorized TikTok account: @retromanymoney. No other users are onboarded or supported. The TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API integrations operate on behalf of that single account exclusively.

3. What it does

  • Reads basic profile information (username, avatar, display name) from the authorized TikTok account.
  • Uploads pre-edited video files from a local machine to the authorized TikTok account, either as drafts (Upload mode) or as direct posts (Direct Post mode), with a caption and hashtags chosen by the channel owner.

4. Acceptable use

The integration is used only for content owned or licensed by the channel owner — short, transformative gameplay clips from retro video games, with on-screen captions and editorial framing. The channel owner is responsible for complying with each game publisher's content guidelines and TikTok's Community Guidelines.
